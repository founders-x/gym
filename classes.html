Classes & Schedule

Six Ways To Train Hard

45+ classes a week across strength, conditioning, and recovery. Find your class below, then check the full weekly schedule.

01

Iron Strength

Our flagship strength-training class. Squat, bench, deadlift, and accessory work programmed in progressive blocks, coached in small groups of 8 or fewer so every rep gets watched.

45 min · All levels
02

HIIT Blast

High-intensity interval circuits mixing kettlebells, sleds, and bodyweight work. Built to spike your heart rate, build conditioning, and torch calories fast.

45 min · All levels
03

Vinyasa Flow Yoga

Breath-led movement and mobility work designed for lifters and athletes who need to recover, stretch, and move pain-free between hard training days.

50 min · All levels
04

Spin Cycle Revolution

Rhythm-based indoor cycling with real power-zone training. Sprints, climbs, and recovery segments set to a coach-curated playlist.

45 min · All levels
05

Boxing Fundamentals

Learn real technique on heavy bags and mitts — stance, footwork, combinations, and defense — in a high-energy hour that doubles as elite conditioning.

50 min · All levels
06

Powerlifting Club

Competition-focused programming for members chasing meet totals, coached by USAPL coach Sam O'Donnell. Squat, bench, and deadlift technique refinement included.

60 min · Intermediate+
