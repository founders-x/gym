About Ironclad

Built By Coaches, Not Just a Gym

Every trainer on our floor is certified, invested, and here because they love watching people get stronger.

Our Story

From One Garage Gym to Tulsa's Training Ground

Ironclad Fitness Studio opened its doors in 2016 in a converted warehouse off Peoria Avenue with six barbells, a stereo, and a promise: no judgment, just work. What started as a small strength-training crew has grown into a full studio offering strength, conditioning, boxing, spin, and yoga — but the promise hasn't changed.

We believe fitness should be motivating, not intimidating. Our coaches program every class with intention, track your progress, and know your name the second time you walk in the door.

Community First

You'll never be just a number here — we build a floor where members push each other.

Coached, Not Just Counted

Every class and every lift is coached for form and progress, not just reps.

Results You Can Measure

Free assessments and body-composition scans keep your progress honest.

Always Improving

New equipment, new programming, and continuing education for every coach, every year.
